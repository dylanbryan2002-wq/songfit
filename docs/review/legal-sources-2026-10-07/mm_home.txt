We bring together creative talent, strategic instinct and the kind of cultural fluency that only comes from being genuinely inside the worlds of music and brand. The result: work that feels local, performs globally and actually moves people.
The Infrastructure Behind It
Our network and technology is rooted in twenty-five years of relationships: artists, rightsholders, global brands, agencies and media. So whatever happens next in music, we're already in the room.
The Only Partner You Need
There's a difference between knowing the music industry and being woven into it. As the only music partner of its kind, MassiveMusic unlocks access, insight and possibility others simply can't. We don't follow where music is going. We're part of how it gets there.
Great brands understand the power of music. That’s why they trust us.

Our Services
Sonic Branding & Design
Elevate your brand with bespoke sonic logos and sound design that connect emotionally and consistently across every consumer touchpoint.
Platform & Music Delivery Services
Deliver, manage, and track music at global scale. Our APIs support metadata, delivery, rights management, reporting and more for platforms, labels, and 1B+ listeners globally.
Supervision & Licensing
From sourcing to clearing rights, our supervision ensures the perfect soundtrack is legally and creatively aligned with your campaign or project.
Research & Insights
Harness the power of cultural and data intelligence to make sound decisions that resonate with your audience. Our music research uncovers emotional impact, creative effectiveness, and audience perception at scale.
Strategy & Consultancy
Craft data-driven music strategies that strengthen brand identity, guide creative direction, and connect with audiences through sound and storytelling.
Brand & Music Compliance Management
Ensure your brand’s sound is consistent, compliant, and protected. Our compliance management platform streamlines approvals, rights, and governance worldwide.
Composition & Production
Create original compositions, sound design, and production for film, TV, advertising, and digital. Crafted to emotionally connect and elevate storytelling.
Reporting Services
Simplify complex rightsholder reporting with secure, automated, end-to-end music and financial reports. Built for accuracy, transparency, and global music compliance.
Activations & Experiential
Create memorable music-led experiences. We help create activations that make your brand unforgettable and engage audiences across the globe.
Metadata Services 
Unlock the power of music with rights-approved metadata, enriched tags, and catalog analysis. Enable enhanced search, improved recommendations, better reporting, and AI applications for platforms globally.
