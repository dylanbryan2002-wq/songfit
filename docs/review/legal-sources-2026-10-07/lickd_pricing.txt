What are track credits?
What are track credits?
Big tracks by famous artists require a one-off license (the only way to make it possible!) Your subscription includes a number of free track credits that you can use on any of these copyrighted songs.
Need more? You can always upgrade your subscription to get more credits, or purchase one-off licenses whenever you need. This starts from as little as $8 and scales depending on how many viewers you have. Which, let’s be honest, is a far cry from the thousands of $$$ you’d pay for traditional music licensing.

How much does a Lickd subscription cost?
How much does a Lickd subscription cost?
Your monthly subscription price depends on two things:
The plan you choose (how many credits you get).
Your Average Video Views on YouTube (AVV).
That last one is based on how traditional music licensing is priced and it levels the playing field so smaller creators don’t have to pay superstar prices. Use the calculator above to see your unique subscription price for each plan.

Why aren’t premium (mainstream) tracks unlimited with the subscription?
Why aren’t premium (mainstream) tracks unlimited with the subscription?
Due to the nature of copyrighted music and music licensing, mainstream tracks can’t be included unlimited with the subscription. The subscription would simply be far too expensive in order to ensure music artists and copyright owners are fairly paid for their work. That defeats the point of Lickd making copyrighted music accessible to all creators!

Can I switch plans anytime?
Can I switch plans anytime?
Absolutely! If you find you need more credits each month you can upgrade your subscription right away so you get access to the credits there and then.
Equally, if you find you’re not using your credits each month you can downgrade your subscription. This will take effect from the next billing date. Oh and don’t worry, you’ll also keep any credits you’ve already received!

Why is the subscription more expensive than other music platforms?
Why is the subscription more expensive than other music platforms?
A Lickd subscription includes track credits to get mainstream, copyrighted music from famous artists which can massively increase your engagement. This higher quality music simply costs more than cheap stock music, which is the only thing other music platforms provide.
We believe in music artists being paid fairly for their work but have also spent a long time negotiating with the music industry to make licenses affordable for creators. If you consider each track starts from about $8 you’re getting a great deal compared to the thousands $$$ you’d pay for traditional music licensing!
Better Music Matters, so see it as an investment in your content and a way to help you grow!!
