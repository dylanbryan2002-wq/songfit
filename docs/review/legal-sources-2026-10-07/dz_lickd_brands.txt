What's included in the Social Sync License
The Social Sync license covers:
Content: Up to 5 short form videos (in total) across TikTok, Instagram Reels, Snapchat & YouTube Shorts for the basic license. There are also affordable options to license up to 55 video usages for Creator-led branded content.
Length: up to 60sec of track use per video
Term: 12 Months
Territory: Worldwide
Paid Boost: 1 Month paid boost per video permitted
Licensed tracks can feature on Brand videos and also Creator videos promoting the Brand.

Will I be charged before my license request is approved?
Will I be charged before my license request is approved?
No, you won’t be charged until your license is completely approved by all rightsholders. We will place a hold for the total amount of the license from your payment card or credit balance while the license request is pending. Once the request is approved, the payment will be automatically processed, and if the license request is declined, the hold and any credit balance will be returned to you.
